# 📖 Glossary – Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This glossary defines important terms used throughout the course.

---

## 🤖 Physical AI
AI systems that operate in the real physical world, interacting with sensors and actuators.

---

## 🧠 Embodied Intelligence
Intelligence that exists within a physical body and understands physical laws.

---

## 📡 ROS 2
Robot Operating System 2 – middleware used to control and coordinate robot software components.

---

## 🔄 Node
A single executable process in ROS 2.

---

## 📢 Topic
A communication channel used by ROS 2 nodes to publish and subscribe to data.

---

## 🔁 Service
A request-response communication model in ROS 2.

---

## 🎯 Action
An asynchronous task in ROS 2 that provides feedback and can be cancelled.

---

## 🏗 URDF
Unified Robot Description Format – XML format describing robot structure and joints.

---

## 🧊 Gazebo
A physics simulation engine used for robot simulation.

---

## 🎮 Unity
A 3D engine used for high-fidelity visualization and interaction.

---

## 🧠 NVIDIA Isaac
A robotics AI platform for perception, simulation, and deployment.

---

## 📍 SLAM
Simultaneous Localization and Mapping – building a map while tracking position.

---

## 📷 VSLAM
Visual SLAM – SLAM using camera-based vision systems.

---

## 🧭 Nav2
ROS 2 navigation framework for autonomous movement.

---

## 🎤 Whisper
OpenAI speech recognition model used for voice-to-text commands.

---

## 🧩 LLM
Large Language Model – used for reasoning and planning tasks.

---

## 🦿 Kinematics
Study of motion without considering forces.

---

## ⚙ Dynamics
Study of forces causing motion.

---

## 🎛 PID Controller
Proportional-Integral-Derivative control system used for stable motion control.

---

## 🧮 MPC
Model Predictive Control – advanced optimization-based control method.

---

## 🖥 Edge Computing
Running AI inference directly on robot hardware (e.g., Jetson).

---

## 🌐 Digital Twin
A virtual replica of a physical robot used for testing and simulation.

---
